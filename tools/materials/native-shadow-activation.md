# Native local spot shadows in the browser

Evidence ID: `BF2-NATIVE-LOCAL-SHADOW-ACTIVATION-001` (`evidence/native-shadow-activation.json`,
regenerated by `python tools/materials/native-shadow-activation-evidence.py`). Runtime:
`web/src/source-shadow-atlas.js`, receivers injected by `web/src/source-light-channels.js`.
`?shadows=0` disables the system.

## What is source-proven

- **Eligibility.** 52 punctual `PbrSpotLightEntityData` records: `CastShadowsEnable`, `CastShadows`
  Low (0) <= native `PunctualLightShadowLevel` 0, `DiscRadius` 0 (`BF2-NATIVE-LOCAL-SHADOW-PRODUCER-001`).
  Spheres and rectangles are all `Disabled`. Near/far, shadow start/end angles, angle ramp and dimmer
  come from `nativePunctualSpotShadowParameters`.
- **Casters.** LOD0 sections in `MeshSubsetCategoryFlags_Shadow` (category 4) from
  `BF2-NATIVE-SHADOW-CASTER-METADATA-001`, published byte for byte by `native-shadow-caster-publish.py`
  (332 assets, 363 sections, 339 of them shadow-only proxies that the visible GLBs omit). All 550 exported
  visible sections are byte-identical to their GLB primitives and no GLB has node transforms, so caster
  world space is exactly `instance.matrix * position`. 13 assets have no shadow section and cast nothing.
- **Depth and filter.** Native reverse depth `A/d+B`, D16 preference with
  `ShaderDepthBiasGroup_Shadow16Bit` (-10, slope -4), comparison `reference > stored`, 4-pixel tile border
  with the expanded caster FOV and the unexpanded receiver matrix, quality-1 nine-gather filter and
  `1+dimmer*ramp*(sample-1)`.

## What is inferred or adapted

- Cross-API bias: under ANGLE D3D11, `polygonOffset(factor, units)` maps to D3D11
  `SlopeScaledDepthBias`/`DepthBias`, which reproduces the native group exactly for D16 (not traced here).
- Caster-pass culling is not recovered; casters are drawn double-sided (avoids light leaking through
  single-sided proxies). Tile allocation order (row-major), the light basis roll and the facing-ramp operand
  (source forward against the surface-to-light direction, as in the native cone term) are inferred.
- Soldiers and the 32 composite meshes (no exported shadow sections) cast with their visible browser
  geometry. Door leaves cast their shadow sections at the current translation.
- **Caching (browser adaptation).** Native `LocalLightShadowCacheEnable` is false. The browser renders a
  light's static casters once into a 32-slot cache atlas (native capacity), copies that tile into a 6-slot
  live atlas and composites dynamic casters there only when something moves inside the light frustum, or
  when an off-screen light re-enters the view. At most one uncached light renders static casters per frame.

## Validation

- `web/test/source-shadow-atlas.test.js`: eligibility (52), slot layout, native depth endpoints, caster pixel
  equals receiver texel including border and flipped rows, LRU cache, payload counts, receiver injection.
- `web/test/source-shadow-atlas-gpu-check.mjs`: real atlas and receivers in a fixture. The static caster
  shadows its light-space footprint (ratio 0), the three mirrored positions stay lit (1.0), the dynamic
  actor proxy casts and clears, and the lit ring shows no acne.
- `web/test/source-shadow-sampling-gpu-check.mjs`: the rolled quality-1 loop still matches unchanged native
  WARP output within 3.121e-7 (12,800 pixels).
- `web/test/actual-map-check.mjs`: shadow stage plus all other stages, no errors.

## Performance notes

- Per-slot data is one std140 uniform block (`BF2SpotShadows`, default binding 0, reserved). As plain
  uniform arrays, three re-uploaded them on every material switch; on the integrated GPU that command
  volume stalled the main thread (44 ms of JS per frame).
- The quality-1 filter is a rolled loop with an arithmetic kernel. Unrolled, its 36 fetches inflated
  register allocation in every receiving shader, costing ~7 ms on the integrated GPU even where unused.
- Shadow receiving costs about 1.1 ms of CPU on the RTX 3050 at spawn (64.8 vs 69.9 FPS) and about
  3.8 ms of GPU on the integrated Radeon (24.5 vs 27.2 FPS). Shader preparation grows from about 6.4 s to
  about 12.3 s cold (warm about 6.5 s). FXC compile time grows with any per-light dependent work in the
  large native light loop; restructuring the receiver (single exit, rolled loops, pre-loop evaluation,
  non-constant loop bounds) did not reduce it.
- `?shadowDiagnostic=no-receivers|no-refresh|no-receiver-programs` isolate the costs.

## Limitations

- Materials already at WebGL's 16 fragment sampler units (Master2UV) cannot bind the atlas and receive no
  local shadows (9 programs at spawn). Freeing a unit would need texture-array packing in that material.
- Only lights in the browser's 6-slot spot pool hold tiles; native per-view caster culling/fade, caster
  LOD selection and entity-level caster overrides are not linked; simple-quality sampling is not wired.
